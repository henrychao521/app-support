HUDCam Support

Applies to: the iPhone app “HUDCam” (bundle ID tw.livingtech.hudcam)　Effective: November 26, 2026　Developer: Henry Chao (individual)

About

Overlay game-style HUDs on your photos and videos — Life Quest, Open World, Racing, Fighter, Shooter, RPG, Pixel Retro, Space Flight, Sports Broadcast, Horror, Rhythm, Power Scanner — plus Flight Telemetry and Time & Place for science projects. Free, no ads, no account.

Basics

  • Change HUD: tap the template button, or swipe left/right on the screen (except in Shooter).
  • Photo / Video: pick a mode and press the shutter. After recording, the HUD is composited and saved to Photos automatically.
  • Review: tap the thumbnail to browse, share, delete, or re-export a video with another HUD.
  • Front/back camera: flip button at the top right. Tap the screen to hide controls.
  • Quality: lens, resolution (720p/1080p/4K), frame rate (24/30/60), codec, stabilization and game sounds in Settings.

Games

  • Life Quest: name tags above people; waving, raising a hand or jumping earns quest rewards.
  • Shooter: joystick moves the crosshair, the fire button shoots; it recognizes what you hit.
  • Fighter: two people, full body in frame, 2–3 m away; punches, kicks, guard and spins are detected.
  • Power Scanner: point at people, pets or objects to see name, rarity and power level.

Flight log and live streaming

  • “Flight Log Mode” (bottom of the template list) records G-force, altitude and GPS at high rate and writes chunks to your own iCloud every 2 seconds. Hold for 3 seconds to stop.
  • Live: enter your YouTube Studio stream URL and key in Settings, then tap the live button.

FAQ

  • Recording stops when locked? iOS doesn't allow camera use in the background; keep the app in front.
  • Phone gets warm? 4K/60 fps with recognition uses more power; the app lowers recognition rate when hot.
  • Where are my files? Original videos and CSV data are in the Files app under HUDCam.
