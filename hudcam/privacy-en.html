HUDCam Privacy Policy

Applies to: the iPhone app “HUDCam” (bundle ID tw.livingtech.hudcam)　Effective: November 26, 2026　Developer: Henry Chao (individual)

HUDCam is a camera app that overlays game-style HUDs and live sensor data on your photos and videos.

No account, no ads, no analytics or tracking SDKs. The developer does not collect, upload, sell or have access to any of your photos, videos, location or data. Face, body-pose and object recognition all run on your device.

1. Photos, videos and data files

Recordings keep a clean original video and a CSV data file in the app's folder (visible in the Files app); exported HUD videos and photos go to Photos. You can delete any item in the app; deleting the app removes its folder (items already saved to Photos stay).

2. On-device recognition and text

Shooter, fighter, action dialogue and scanner features use Apple Vision and a built-in on-device detection model; no images are uploaded. Result-card comments are generated by iOS's on-device model on supported devices.

3. Flight log and iCloud

Only while a flight log is running, sensor data is written every 2 seconds to your own iCloud private database so you can recover it from another device. The developer cannot access it; manage or delete it in Settings → your Apple Account → iCloud.

4. Live streaming

Optional. Video and audio go directly from your phone to the streaming URL you enter (e.g. YouTube) and are handled under that platform's privacy policy. Your stream key stays in the device Keychain.

5. Children

No account and no personal information is collected. The app is not directed to children; do not use live streaming if you are under 13.

Changes will be posted here with a new effective date. Questions: henrychao521@gmail.com.
