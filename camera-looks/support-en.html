iPhone App “Camera Looks Notebook” (相機配方本)

About the App

Camera Looks Notebook is a pocket reference app designed for photographers who enjoy in-camera picture looks. It compiles picture recipes that can be configured directly on camera bodies (a total of 93 recipes across three camera models).

All setting parameters are arranged strictly following the camera body menu order, allowing you to enter settings step by step without searching back and forth. The app includes a large-text “In-Shooting Mode” where the screen stays awake while the screen is open, favorites, recently viewed history (up to 30 items), and Home Screen widgets. Each recipe includes the original creator’s name and a link to the original post (sample photos from original creators are not included).

System Requirements

  • iPhone running iOS 17 or later.
  • No external hardware, camera connection, or user account required. All features are fully functional immediately upon install.

Frequently Asked Questions

IssueSolution
How do I add a recipe to the Home Screen widget?Pin the desired recipe inside the app. Then, long-press empty space on your iPhone Home Screen to enter jiggle mode, tap the “+” button in the top corner, search for “Camera Looks Notebook” (相機配方本), and add the widget.
Why does the screen not turn off in “In-Shooting Mode”?“In-Shooting Mode” is designed specifically for configuring camera menus. The screen will not turn off automatically while this screen is open so you can adjust camera settings without interruption; leaving the screen restores normal auto-lock behavior.
Are sample photos from original creators included?To respect copyright, the app only compiles text parameters and creator credits; sample photos are not included. You can tap “Open Original in Browser” (在瀏覽器打開原文) on any recipe to visit the creator’s website and see full sample images.
Will my favorites and history be preserved when switching devices?No. The app has no servers and does not use iCloud sync. All data (favorites, up to 30 recent items, pinned widgets) is stored strictly on this device via an App Group. Deleting the app removes all stored data.
Does the app require camera connection or any permissions?Not at all. The app requests no system permissions and does not connect to camera hardware. All parameters are for manual input on your camera body.
