Camera Looks Notebook Privacy Policy

Applies to: iPhone app “Camera Looks Notebook” (相機配方本)　Effective date: October 22, 2026　Contact: henrychao521@gmail.com

Camera Looks Notebook is a companion reference app that organizes picture recipes designed to be set directly on camera bodies (a total of 93 recipes across three camera models). The app organizes parameters according to the camera body menu order, featuring a large-text “In-Shooting Mode”, favorites, and Home Screen widgets.

The app has no servers, requires no account registration or login, and does not collect, upload, or sell any personal data. It contains no advertisements and no analytics or tracking SDKs. The app itself does not connect to the internet, and all data is stored exclusively on your device. Below is a detailed description of what data the app touches and how it is handled.

1. System Permissions

The app requests no system permissions and collects no personal data.

2. Data Stored on Device

The app stores only the following data locally on your device:

  • Favorites
  • Recently viewed history (up to 30 items)
  • Recipes pinned to widgets

The app and its Home Screen widgets share this data locally on the same device via an App Group so that the widgets can display your pinned recipes. The app does not use iCloud sync and does not share any data with third parties. When you delete the app from your device, all stored data is permanently deleted. The developer has no servers and cannot access any of this data.

3. External Links

Each recipe includes the original author’s name and a link to the original post (sample photos from original creators are not included). Only when you actively tap “Open Original in Browser” (在瀏覽器打開原文) or “Recipe Website” (配方本網站) will the app open the external link using the system browser. Those external websites are governed by their own independent privacy policies and terms.

4. Sharing

You can share recipe text using the iOS system share sheet. The method and recipients of your sharing are entirely decided and performed by you. The app does not track, monitor, or transmit your sharing content or recipients.

5. Screen Display (In-Shooting Mode)

When the large-text “In-Shooting Mode” screen is active, the screen will not turn off automatically so that you can view parameters while configuring camera menus. As soon as you leave that screen, standard system auto-lock behavior resumes. This feature is purely a local screen state control and involves no personal data processing.

6. Trademarks and Disclaimer

All camera brand and model names are the property of their respective owners. This app is independent and is not affiliated with, authorized by, or sponsored by any camera manufacturer.

7. Policy Changes and Contact

If this policy changes, this page and the effective date above will be updated. For questions or suggestions, please contact henrychao521@gmail.com.
