QiLing (棋靈) Support

Developer: Henry Chao. Contact: henrychao521@gmail.com

What you can do

  • Play computer opponents at gomoku, chess, xiangqi, banqi and Go. Each game offers opponents of different strengths; Go supports 9×9, 13×13 and 19×19 boards, ranks from 20 kyu to 9 dan, handicap stones and komi.
  • Lessons: unit-based exercises for gomoku and chess. Correct answers earn stars and unlock the next lesson.
  • Game records: every game is saved automatically. Replay move by move, auto-play, resume, and export or import (SGF for Go and gomoku, PGN for chess, text notation for xiangqi and banqi). The latest 500 games are kept; starred games are never removed.
  • Play with friends: Game Center turn-based matches. Create a room and give your friend the 6-digit room code (gomoku, chess, xiangqi).
  • Spectating: turn on "Allow spectators" in a remote game to get an 8-digit watch code. Family and friends enter it under Play with friends › Watch; the board refreshes about every 6 seconds.

FAQ

QuestionAnswer
Why does Go take a few seconds the first time?After the first install or an update the opponent needs a few seconds to get ready. Afterwards it is usually ready within a second or two.
How is a Go game scored?Chinese area scoring with 7.5 komi. After both sides pass, the scoring screen marks the stones judged dead; tap to correct them, then confirm.
When is a xiangqi game drawn?When the same position occurs for the third time (if one side caused it by checking every move, that side loses), or after 120 moves without a capture.
I cannot play with a friend.Check that you are signed in under Settings › Game Center and that both devices are online.
I cannot turn spectating on.Uploading a game requires the device to be signed in to iCloud. Viewers do not need to sign in.
The watch code is not found.Check the 8 digits. A game is no longer visible once a player turns spectating off, or 7 days after it ended.
Does the app go online or collect data?Playing, lessons and records stay on the device. Only Game Center (Play with friends) and iCloud (spectating) use the network. See the privacy policy.
