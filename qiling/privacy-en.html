QiLing (棋靈) Privacy Policy

Applies to the iPhone/iPad app "棋靈" (bundle ID tw.livingtech.qiling). Effective 26 November 2026. Developer: Henry Chao (individual).

QiLing is a board-game app. You can play gomoku, chess, xiangqi (Chinese chess), banqi (dark chess) and Go against computer opponents of different strengths, work through lessons, play friends remotely and let family or friends watch a game online.

The app has no account of its own, no ads, and no analytics or tracking SDKs. It does not ask for camera, microphone, location or notification permission. All move calculation and dialogue run on your device; games are not sent to any server. Only two Apple services are used over the network: remote play with friends (Apple Game Center) and spectating (Apple iCloud).

1. Play with friends (Game Center)

Remote play uses Apple Game Center. Whether you sign in and whether your nickname and profile are public are your choices in Settings › Game Center; Game Center handles data under Apple's privacy policy. At launch the app asks Game Center whether you are signed in (standard Game Center behaviour). Everything else — playing, lessons and records — works without signing in, and moves are sent only when you use Play with friends.

2. Spectating (iCloud)

  • Off by default. When you turn on "Allow spectators" in a remote game, the app creates an 8-digit watch code and uploads the game's moves, game settings, status and result to the iCloud public database. A watch code works like a public link: anyone who knows it can see the moves of that game.
  • Nicknames are not uploaded by default; viewers see "Black/White" or "Red/Black". A nickname is uploaded only if you tick "Show my nickname". The app never uploads your Game Center player ID, Apple Account, email address or contacts.
  • If your opponent turns spectating on, you see a notice. You may choose "Don't share mine": your device then uploads nothing and your nickname is not shown, but your opponent's device still uploads the full list of moves.
  • Uploading requires the device to be signed in to iCloud. iCloud records which iCloud user created each record (an anonymous identifier without your name) so that only the creator can change or delete it; the app does not read or display that identifier.
  • When you turn spectating off, the app deletes the record you uploaded; records are also deleted automatically the next time the app starts once a game has been finished for 7 days. Records that viewers already saved to their own on-device library are not deleted.
  • Watching needs no sign-in. There is no chat, no comments and no viewer list; the app does not record who watched which game.
  • To delete the cloud record earlier, turn "Allow spectators" off in the game. If you have already removed the app, email the developer with the watch code and the record will be deleted for you.

3. On-device processing

Move selection, position evaluation and scoring are computed on the device. On some models the opponents' short remarks use the system's built-in on-device language feature, which also works offline; other models use fixed built-in lines.

4. Children's privacy

The app needs no account and collects no name, contact details or other personally identifying data. It has no ads and no in-app purchases. Whether remote play and spectating are available is controlled by parents through Game Center and iCloud settings.

5. Third-party components

The app includes open-source components; their licence notices are listed in the app under Settings › Licences. These components run only on the device, do not use the network and collect no data.
