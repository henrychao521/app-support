PaiLing (牌靈) Privacy Policy

Applies to the iPhone/iPad app "PaiLing" (牌靈). Developer: Henry Chao (individual).

PaiLing is a card-game app where you play Pick Red (撿紅點, 2–4 players), Big Two (4 players) and Bridge (bidding, play and duplicate scoring) against computer opponents of four ranks: Xiaoye (小葉), Asong (阿松), Qinglan (青嵐) and Molao (墨老).

The app works completely offline. It does not connect to the network, requires no account, has no coins, no betting, no in-app purchases and no ads, and uses no third-party analytics or advertising SDKs. We do not collect any personal data, do not track you and do not send any data to any server.

1. Offline operation and no data collection

All gameplay and data storage happen on your device. PaiLing does not connect to the network, has no account system, collects no personal data, does not track you, sends no data to any server and uses no third-party analytics or advertising SDKs.

2. Data storage and deletion

Settings, rule options, statistics and auto-saved unfinished games stay only on your device. You can clear your statistics in Settings at any time. Deleting the app removes all data from your device.

3. Children's privacy

The app needs no account, collects no personal data and has no ads, coins, betting or in-app purchases.

4. Changes and contact

If this policy changes, this page will be updated. Questions: henrychao521@gmail.com (developer: Henry Chao).
