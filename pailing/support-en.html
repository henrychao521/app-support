PaiLing (牌靈) Support

Developer: Henry Chao. Contact: henrychao521@gmail.com

What you can do

  • Play computer opponents in Pick Red (撿紅點, 2–4 players), Big Two (4 players) and Bridge (bidding, play and duplicate scoring). Four opponent ranks are available: Xiaoye (小葉), Asong (阿松), Qinglan (青嵐) and Molao (墨老).
  • Auto-save and statistics: unfinished games are saved automatically on your device so you can resume them from the home screen. Games played and wins for each game type and rank are recorded locally and can be cleared in Settings.
  • Completely offline: no account required, no network connection, no coins, no betting, no in-app purchases and no ads.

FAQ

QuestionAnswer
How do I resume my last game?Unfinished games are saved automatically. Tap "Continue Last Game" on the home screen to pick up where you left off.
Can I adjust Big Two rules?You can adjust 7 rule options in Settings: flushes count as a hand, triples can be played alone, a higher five-card hand type can beat a lower one, four of a kind and straight flushes can beat singles and pairs, straight ranking, no 2 on the last play, and double penalty for each 2 left in hand.
How does Bridge play and what does Suggest do?You sit South. When North-South declares, you play both declarer and dummy. Tapping "Suggest" shows what a bid means.
What rule options are available for Pick Red?You can adjust three rules: must capture when possible, the Ace of spades and Ace of clubs score points, and the red-5 bonus when a red 5 captures a red 5 in Settings.
Does the app go online, offer purchases or collect data?No. PaiLing runs completely offline with no coins, betting, in-app purchases or ads, requires no account and does not collect or send data. See the privacy policy.
How do I clear my statistics or all data?You can clear your statistics in Settings. Deleting the app removes all data from your device.
