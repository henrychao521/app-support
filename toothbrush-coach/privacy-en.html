Brushing Coach Privacy Policy

Applies to: the iOS app “Brushing Coach” (潔牙小教練)　Effective: October 22, 2026　Developer: Henry Chao (individual)

“Brushing Coach” is an app for practicing tooth brushing sequence and technique. Using the front camera, the app determines the position of the toothbrush in the mouth in real time, guides users through 6 mouth zones in order, and provides scores and missed-zone feedback upon completion.

This app operates completely offline without its own server. It requires no account, involves no login, displays no advertisements, contains no analytics or tracking SDKs, shares no data with third parties, and does not use iCloud sync. All data is kept strictly on your local device and is completely erased when the app is deleted. The details below explain what data the app interacts with and how privacy is protected.

1. Face Data & Camera Processing (Important Notice)

The app utilizes Apple ARKit face tracking (TrueDepth front camera) and Vision hand detection technologies:

  • Real-time On-device Processing: Face and hand positions are calculated strictly on your local device in real time to determine toothbrush positioning and motion.
  • No Video Recording & No Photos: The app does not record video, does not take photos, and does not capture any static or dynamic imagery.
  • No Storage & No Uploading: The app does not store facial data or camera frames. Data is discarded from memory immediately after computation and is never transmitted to any server.
  • No Sharing & No Identification: Face data is never shared with anyone or any third party, is never used for identity verification or facial recognition, and is not used for any other purpose.
  • Permission Handling: An explanatory screen is displayed before requesting camera access. If camera permission is denied, users can still fully access the Tutorial and Demo Mode.

2. Data Stored on the Device

The app stores only the following essential information locally in its sandbox container:

  • Practice length (short / standard / full).
  • Brushing records: Includes session date, score, and seconds spent in each zone, up to a maximum of 200 records.
  • Consecutive brushing streak (records from “Demo Mode” do not count toward streaks).
  • Voice toggle and custom brushing sequence.

This data is not synced via iCloud and cannot be accessed by the developer or any third party. Deleting the app from your device permanently removes all stored data.

3. Voice Synthesis

Spoken prompts are generated locally on the device using the built-in iOS speech synthesis engine. No internet connection is used, and no requests are made to external speech services.

4. Records & Settings, External Actions

  • Records & Settings: Shows practice history and lets you change the practice length, set a custom brushing order, toggle voice prompts, and clear records. All changes stay on the device.
  • External Links & Navigation: The app contains no web browsers, external links, or social media sharing features. The only external action is opening iOS Settings when the user taps “Open Settings” to enable camera permission.

5. No Personal Data Collection

The app does not collect personal data from anyone, and contains no ads, trackers, or account systems.

6. Disclaimer

The app is intended solely for practicing brushing sequence and technique. It does not provide medical advice, makes no claims regarding prevention or treatment, and cannot replace regular examinations, diagnoses, or advice from a licensed dentist.

7. Policy Changes & Contact

If this privacy policy is updated, changes will be reflected on this page along with the revised effective date. For any questions or inquiries regarding this policy, please contact: henrychao521@gmail.com (Developer: Henry Chao).
