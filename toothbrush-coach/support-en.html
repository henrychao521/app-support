Brushing Coach Support & FAQ

iOS App “Brushing Coach” (潔牙小教練): Brushing sequence and technique practice

About the App

“Brushing Coach” helps you practice tooth brushing sequence and technique. By utilizing the front camera, the app determines the position of the toothbrush in the mouth in real time, guiding users step-by-step through 6 distinct mouth zones and providing scores and missed-zone feedback upon completion. It also features a cartoon-animated Demo Mode, tutorials, and a Records & Settings page.

System Requirements

  • Operating System: iOS 17 or later.
  • Real-time Detection: Requires an iPhone equipped with a Face ID front camera (TrueDepth camera system).
  • Other Devices: Devices without a TrueDepth camera, or with camera permissions denied, can fully access “Demo Mode” and “Tutorials”.

Frequently Asked Questions

QuestionDetails & Troubleshooting
Why is real-time brushing detection unavailable?Real-time detection requires iOS 17 or later on an iPhone with a Face ID front camera (TrueDepth), along with granted camera permission. If permission was denied, you can tap the in-app prompt to open System Settings and enable camera access. Devices without TrueDepth can still use “Demo Mode”.
Does the app record, store, or share facial images?No. The app uses ARKit face tracking and Vision hand detection strictly on-device in real time to calculate position coordinates. It does not record video, take photos, store facial data or images, upload data, or share anything with anyone. It is never used for personal identification.
What is “Demo Mode”, and does it count toward streaks?Demo Mode features cartoon animations with synthetic motions to demonstrate brushing steps without using the camera. Sessions completed in Demo Mode do not count toward consecutive brushing streaks.
What is the practice length, and where do I change it?The practice length sets the guided and scored time per region: short (10 seconds), standard (12 seconds, default), or full (15 seconds). Change it on the home screen or in “Records & Settings”.
What is in “Records & Settings”?Recent scores, most-missed regions, and your practice streak, plus settings for practice length, custom brushing order, voice prompts, and clearing records.
Will brushing history be retained if I switch phones or delete the app?No. To ensure maximum privacy, the app operates offline without a server and does not use iCloud sync. All data (practice length, up to 200 brushing logs, streaks) resides exclusively on your local device and is permanently removed when the app is deleted.
