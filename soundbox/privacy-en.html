SoundBox Privacy Policy

Applies to: the iPhone / iPad and Apple Watch app “SoundBox” (Chinese name 音效盒, bundle ID tw.livingtech.soundbox)　Effective: November 8, 2026　Developer: Henry Chao (individual)

SoundBox is a box of sound effects (gunshots, farts, canned laughter, cats and dogs, snoring and more) that you can play by tapping, shaking or with a countdown. You can also record your own sounds.

The app does not connect to the internet, has no account, no ads and no analytics or tracking SDKs. It does not collect, upload, sell or share any data, and the developer cannot see any of your data.

The optional “buy the developer a coffee” tip is processed by Apple In-App Purchase. The developer never receives your payment details, and a tip unlocks nothing.

1. Microphone and recordings

  • Microphone permission is requested only the first time you press Record. All built-in sounds work without it.
  • Recordings stay inside the app on this device. They are never uploaded or analyzed. Delete any recording on the Record tab; deleting the app deletes all recordings.

2. Motion sensor

Shake-to-play on iPhone and wrist-flick on Apple Watch read the current acceleration only to decide whether to play. Motion data is not recorded or stored. You can turn this off in Settings on the About tab.

3. Widgets, Control Center and Apple Watch

Home Screen widgets, the Control Center control and the Apple Watch app use only the built-in sounds and your own choices. They do not connect to the internet or send data.

4. Children’s privacy

No account is needed and no names, contact details or other personal data are collected. When a child taps a tip, Apple’s Ask to Buy lets a parent approve it.

5. Changes and contact

If this policy changes, this page and the effective date above will be updated. Questions: henrychao521@gmail.com (developer: Henry Chao).
