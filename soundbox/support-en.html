SoundBox Help & Support

Applies to: the iPhone / iPad and Apple Watch app “SoundBox” (Chinese name 音效盒, bundle ID tw.livingtech.soundbox)　Effective: November 8, 2026　Developer: Henry Chao (individual)

About the app

A box of sound effects: tap to play, tap repeatedly to layer, shake to play, or set a countdown. Free, no ads, no account, works offline and collects no data.

How to use

  • Tap: pick a category on the Sounds tab and tap a tile. Rapid taps layer the sound.
  • Long press: long-press a tile to trigger it by shaking or by countdown.
  • Shake: turn on the master switch and set the sensitivity on the About tab. Gunshots come with haptics.
  • Countdown: keep the app in the foreground. If it goes to the background, a notification reminds you to come back.
  • Record: record your own sounds on the Record tab and use them like built-in sounds.
  • Widgets and Control Center: long-press the Home Screen, tap Edit, add a SoundBox widget, then long-press it to choose sounds.
  • Apple Watch: pick a category and tap a tile to play through the watch speaker, or flick your wrist.

FAQ

  • No sound? The silent switch does not mute the sounds (that is the point of a sound effects app), but volume at 0 does. Turn on the zero-volume reminder on the About tab.
  • Shaking does nothing? Check that both the master shake switch and the sound’s shake setting are on, or raise the sensitivity.
  • Where do the sounds come from? The About tab lists every source. Sounds marked as AI-generated were made with Stable Audio Open 1.0 (Powered by Stability AI).
  • Refund a tip? Apple handles payment. Request refunds at reportaproblem.apple.com.
