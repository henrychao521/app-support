Pencil Grip Coach App Privacy Policy

Applies to: iOS App “Pencil Grip Coach” (握筆小教練) | Effective Date: October 22, 2026 | Developer: Henry Chao (Individual)

Pencil Grip Coach helps elementary school students practice pencil grip posture. It uses the camera to detect hand keypoints, evaluate grip posture, and provide easy-to-understand feedback, along with tutorials and practice logs.

This app operates completely offline without servers, requires no account or login, contains no advertisements, includes no analytics or tracking SDKs, does not share data with third parties, and does not use iCloud synchronization. All data stays solely on this device and is permanently removed when the app is deleted. Details on permissions and data handling are provided below.

1. Camera Permission and Image Processing

The app requests camera access to detect hand keypoints:

  • Notice Before Prompt: The app displays an explanatory screen before requesting system camera permission. If permission is denied, tutorial and demo modes remain fully accessible.
  • On-Device Real-Time Processing: Video frames are analyzed in real time solely to detect hand keypoints and are immediately discarded.
  • No Video Recording or Photos: The app does not record videos and does not take or save photos.
  • No Uploads or Sharing: All frames and keypoints remain entirely on the device, are never uploaded to any server, and are never shared with any third party.

2. Data Stored on Device

The app stores practice records locally:

  • Numerical Records Only: Records contain only numbers (duration in seconds and percentages of each grip posture), without any photos or images, keeping up to 200 entries.
  • Device-Only Storage: Does not use iCloud sync and has no external server.
  • Complete Deletion: All stored records are completely deleted when the app is removed from the device.

3. Demo Mode

The app includes a built-in demo mode using synthetic hand animations to demonstrate pencil grip postures, allowing full exploration without needing a camera.

4. Connectivity and Third-Party Services

  • Completely Offline: The app does not connect to the internet and operates without a backend server.
  • No Ads or Tracking: Contains no advertising and no analytics or tracking SDKs.
  • No Data Sharing: No information is shared with or sold to third parties.

5. Children's Privacy

The app does not collect personal information from children or any other users, and never asks for identifying details.

6. Disclaimer

Posture evaluation is based on geometric rules calculated from hand keypoint distances. Results are for practice guidance only and may be affected by lighting, distance, or angle. The app is not a medical evaluation.

Any updates to this policy will be posted on this page with an updated effective date. For questions or support, contact developer email: henrychao521@gmail.com (Developer: Henry Chao).
