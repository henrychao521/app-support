Pencil Grip Coach Support

iOS App “Pencil Grip Coach” (握筆小教練): Pencil Grip Guidance and Practice

About the App

Pencil Grip Coach is designed for elementary students to practice proper pencil grip postures. Using the device camera, it detects hand keypoints in real time to recognize grip postures (thumb wrapping index finger, thumb tucked in, gripping too low, gripping too loose, or excellent grip), providing friendly suggestions along with tutorials and practice logs.

System Requirements

  • Operating System: iOS 17 or later.
  • Camera: Required for real-time grip posture evaluation.
  • Tutorial & Demo Mode: Can be used without camera access or on devices without camera.
  • Internet & Accounts: Operates completely offline; no account or login required.

Frequently Asked Questions

QuestionAnswer
How accurate is the grip posture evaluation?Evaluations are based on geometric rules calculated from hand keypoint distances. Results are for practice guidance only and may misclassify due to lighting, angle, or distance; this is not a medical evaluation.
Are camera video frames stored or uploaded?No. Frames are processed entirely on-device in real time to detect hand keypoints and are immediately discarded. The app does not record video, does not save photos, does not upload to servers, and does not share data.
Can I use the app without granting camera permission?Yes. If camera permission is denied, you can still view the tutorials and explore grip postures using Demo Mode with synthetic hand animations.
Where are practice records stored, and do they contain photos?Records are stored only locally on your device. They consist purely of numbers (percentages of each grip type and practice seconds), contain no photos, and keep up to 200 entries. They are permanently deleted if the app is removed.
Is an internet connection required?No. The app does not connect to the internet and contains no backend server. All computing is performed on-device.
