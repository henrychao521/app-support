Physics Interactive Lab (物理互動實驗室) Privacy Policy

Applies to: the iOS app “Physics Interactive Lab (物理互動實驗室)” (Bundle ID: tw.livingtech.physicslab)　Effective: October 30, 2026　Developer: Henry Chao (individual)

Physics Interactive Lab is an interactive physics learning tool designed for junior and senior high school students. It covers dozens of interactive physics simulations across six thematic halls: Mechanics, Thermodynamics, Waves, Optics, Electromagnetism, and Modern Physics. This includes 3 native SwiftUI/RealityKit 3D experiments (Surgical Shadowless Lamp with umbra/penumbra, illuminance heatmap, 2D cross-section, and optional tabletop AR; Wave Diffraction & Interference; and Moiré Pattern with theoretical periods), 5 native real-measurement experiments utilizing the microphone and motion sensors (Air Column Resonance Tube, Doppler Effect, Simple Harmonic Motion, Uniform Circular Motion, and Inclined Plane), a Video Analysis tool that tracks moving objects frame by frame in videos (with 8 experiment templates), and 63 bundled offline web simulations, presenting physical principles through interactive visualizations.

The app itself does not connect to the internet, operates without any server, requires no account, does not collect, upload, or sell any personal data, and contains no ads, analytics, or tracking SDKs. It shares no data with third parties. The app embeds no external web pages; the few links to outside websites ask first and only open in Safari (leaving the app) after you tap “Open”. Below are the details regarding permissions and data handling.

1. Camera Permission: Augmented Reality (AR) and Video Analysis

  • Requested only when you act: The app requests camera permission only when you open AR mode in the surgical shadowless lamp experiment (on an AR-capable device), or when you tap “Record” in Video Analysis.
  • Two purposes: In AR mode, camera frames are used to detect a flat tabletop surface in real time and place the shadowless lamp 3D scene onto it. In Video Analysis, the camera records the motion you want to analyze.
  • AR is processed in real time without storage: Camera frames in AR mode are processed in real time strictly on-device and are never photographed, recorded, stored, or uploaded.
  • Video Analysis records only when you tap Record: Recordings contain video only; the microphone is not used and no audio is recorded. Videos are stored in the app's own folder (they are not saved to Photos automatically) and are used only to track the objects you select, frame by frame, on this device. Results (CSV, PNG) are handed to the iOS share sheet only when you tap “Export”. You can delete videos by swiping left in Video Analysis → My Videos.
  • Importing from Photos: Importing uses the system photo picker; the app receives only a copy of the one video you choose and cannot read your photo library, so no Photos permission is requested.
  • Optional features: AR and Video Analysis are both optional. The shadowless lamp experiment provides complete 3D interactive views and 2D cross-sections, and Video Analysis can also use videos imported from Photos or the built-in sample videos. Denying camera permission does not affect any other feature or simulation.

2. Microphone Permission, Motion Sensors, and Haptics

  • Requested only upon active measurement: Microphone permission is requested only in the "Air Column Resonance Tube" and "Doppler Effect" experiments when the user explicitly taps "Start Measurement".
  • Real-time frequency analysis without recording: Audio is processed strictly in volatile memory in real time for Fast Fourier Transform (FFT) frequency analysis. The app does not record audio, save audio files, or upload any sound to servers. Video Analysis does not use the microphone when recording.
  • Simulated audio source alternative: Microphone usage is entirely optional. If permission is denied or the device lacks a microphone, users can switch to the in-app "Simulated Audio Source" with full functionality.
  • Motion sensors (accelerometer & gyroscope): Standard motion sensors are used for physical measurements in Simple Harmonic Motion, Uniform Circular Motion, and Inclined Plane experiments. iOS does not require a permission prompt for these sensors. Data is kept in volatile memory only for the most recent 12 seconds and is never saved or uploaded.
  • Haptic feedback: Subtle vibrations are triggered locally when a period is measured or when crossing the critical angle.

3. Data Stored on the Device

All app data is stored exclusively on your local device:

  • Preferences and progress: Favorites, recently opened items (up to 20), hall exploration progress (launch counts, duration seconds, operation counts), and interface preference settings are stored locally in UserDefaults.
  • Web simulation cache: The pre-bundled web simulations are cached locally by system WebKit for offline execution.
  • Video Analysis videos: Videos you record or import are stored in the app's Documents folder until you delete them in the app or delete the app.

The app does not use iCloud synchronization and maintains no remote servers or databases. All data remains strictly on this device and is entirely deleted by the operating system when the app is removed. The developer cannot access or read this data.

4. Network Connections, Permissions, and Third Parties

  • Completely offline and serverless: All simulations (both the native experiments and the web simulations) are bundled directly inside the app package. They run completely offline without an internet connection or backend servers.
  • External links: The app embeds no external web pages and has no in-app browser. The few external links inside simulations (e.g. “search external source data” on the hovercraft page) first ask “Open external website in Safari?”; only after you tap “Open” does Safari open it and you leave the app. Tapping “Cancel” does nothing. External sites are responsible for their own content and privacy; the app sends them no data.
  • Unused sensitive permissions: The app does not access or request permissions for location, contacts, health data, or Bluetooth, and it never reads your photo library. Importing a video from Photos uses the system picker and gives the app only the one video you choose.
  • Audio playback: Simulations such as the Sound Wave Synthesizer utilize Web Audio, and simulated audio sources for Doppler and resonance tube use system audio to synthesize sound locally on the device for real-time playback only. The app does not record audio or upload sound.
  • Image downloads and printing: When you tap “Download PNG” or “Print” in a simulation, the image or PDF is created on your device and handed to the iOS share sheet, where you choose whether to save it to Photos or Files, send it via AirDrop, or share it. The app never sends it automatically. The first time you choose “Save Image” in the share sheet, iOS asks whether the app may add images to Photos; the app only adds the image you chose to save and never reads your photo library.
  • No ads or tracking: The app does not integrate any advertising, analytics, or tracking SDKs.
  • No third-party sharing: The app does not collect personal data and does not share, transmit, or sell any data to third parties.

5. Children and Student Privacy

This app is designed for junior and senior high school students. It does not require account creation or login, and it does not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, unique device identifiers, or any personally identifiable information.

6. Educational Model Disclaimer

All simulations in this app use simplified physical models for educational purposes, with parameter scales adjusted for educational demonstrations. The surgical shadowless lamp simulation is an educational model and not medical device software; its values differ from actual clinical equipment specifications and must not be used as a basis for medical device procurement or clinical use.
