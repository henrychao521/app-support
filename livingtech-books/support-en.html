LivingTech Interactive Lab Support

Applies to: the iPhone and iPad app "LivingTech Interactive Lab" (Chinese name: 生科互動實驗室, bundle ID: tw.livingtech.books)　Last updated: November 13, 2026　Developer: Henry Chao (individual)

About the App

"LivingTech Interactive Lab" contains 140 interactive modules for junior and senior high school Living Technology and Engineering Design classes. They are arranged by the learning content of the Technology domain in Taiwan's 108 Curriculum Guidelines into five groups (Grade 7, Grade 8, Grade 9, Required, and Advanced Elective) and come with lesson animations. The interface is in Traditional Chinese.

The native interface provides a curriculum map, search, favorites (up to 300), recently opened items and learning progress. The interactive modules, the first chapter of every lesson animation and the Python runtime (Pyodide) are bundled in the app, work offline, and run only on this device. The content is written by the developer, who is the author of the learning materials; materials from partner organizations are used with permission, and image and data credits are listed in the app under "About & Privacy → Licenses and credits".

System Requirements

  • iOS 17 or later, on iPhone and iPad. Apple Intelligence feedback on open-ended questions needs a supported device with iOS 26 or later; other devices use built-in rule-based feedback.
  • No account and no sign-in. The only system permission is "Add to Photos", requested only when you choose "Save Image" in the share sheet.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I watch the lesson animations? Where is the full version?

The first chapter of every lesson animation is bundled in the app. Tap a cover in the "Animations" tab or inside a unit to play it full screen with no network connection. The full versions are on YouTube: open the unit that contains the animation and tap "Watch the full version (YouTube)". This leaves the app, opens YouTube and needs a network connection.

Are the lesson animations made with AI?

The narration voices, character animation and background music are AI-generated. The scripts and teaching content are written and reviewed by the developer, who is the author of the learning materials. If you find a mistake, please email us.

What is "More learning resources"?

"Mine → More learning resources" lists other free educational apps by the same developer. "View in App Store" only opens the App Store product page; nothing is downloaded automatically and the app does not record what you tap. "Related physics experiments" at the top of some modules works the same way: if the developer's "Physics Lab" app is installed it opens the matching experiment there, otherwise its App Store product page opens.

Why don't external videos or links inside a module open directly?

The app does not embed external web pages. External videos and external interactive websites that were embedded in a module are replaced by an information card. Links to external websites (for example, further resources and sources) first ask whether to open them in Safari; after you tap "Open", you leave the app and Safari shows the page. Those websites have their own privacy policies.

Why does the map in "Taiwan Engineering Landscapes" sometimes show a blank grid?

The modules and photos are bundled, but the basemap needs a network connection to download tiles from the National Land Surveying and Mapping Center (Ministry of the Interior, Taiwan) and Esri map servers. Offline, the map shows a blank grid and everything else works as usual.

Are my answers uploaded? What is "Hand in to teacher"?

By default nothing is uploaded; answers stay on this device. "Hand in to teacher" is optional and off by default. Only after a teacher opens "About & Privacy → Teacher settings" on this device, enters the teacher passcode, enters the receiving URL of the teacher's own Google Sheet and turns the feature on do open-ended questions show a "Hand in to teacher" button. When a student taps it, the question, the answer, the analysis marks, the time and the optional class and seat number are sent to the teacher's own Google Sheet. Names are not sent, and the developer never receives the data. See the Privacy Policy for details.

Does "AI feedback" send my answer over the internet?

No. When you tap "AI feedback", the answer is analyzed only on this device: Apple's on-device model on devices with Apple Intelligence, built-in rules on other devices. AI feedback is only a learning hint; it may be wrong and it is not a grade.

What is "Buy the developer a coffee"? Do I have to tip?

No. Everything in the app is free. A tip is voluntary and unlocks nothing, and students do not need to tip. Tips use Apple's In-App Purchase; Apple processes the payment and the developer never receives your payment details. If a child's account uses Ask to Buy, the purchase completes only after a parent approves it.

Can a tip be refunded?

Apple processes the payment. Request a refund from Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

How can I save, print or share worksheets or my work?

Inside modules, "Print" creates a PDF, "Download" creates a file, and "Share" passes text or a URL. All three open the iOS share sheet so you can save to Files, send by AirDrop, print or share with others. "Copy" only places content on the system clipboard. The app itself never sends anything automatically.

Where are my progress and answers stored, and how do I clear them?

Opened and completed modules, favorites (up to 300), recently opened items, in-page interaction counts and each module's answer progress are stored only on this device, with no iCloud sync, and the developer cannot read them. Under "Mine → About & Privacy", "Clear all learning progress" clears opened, completed and recently opened items (favorites are kept). Deleting the app deletes everything.
