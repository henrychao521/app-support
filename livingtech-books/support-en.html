LivingTech Interactive Lab Support

Applies to: iPhone and iPad App "LivingTech Interactive Lab" (Chinese name: 生科互動實驗室, Bundle ID: tw.livingtech.books)　Effective Date: October 27, 2026　Developer: Henry Chao (Individual)

About the App

"LivingTech Interactive Lab" brings together interactive modules from five living technology and engineering curriculum websites into a single App: Junior High Living Technology, Senior High Living Technology, Senior High Engineering Design, Taiwan Engineering Landscapes, and Circuit Practice Labs, plus a "Python Coding Dojo".

The native interface provides topic categories, search, favorites (up to 300 items), recently opened items, and learning progress. All curriculum content and the Python execution environment (Pyodide) for the Python Coding Dojo are bundled inside the App for offline use, and code runs strictly on this device. Curriculum content copyrights belong to the respective publishers and original authors and are included with authorization.

System Requirements

  • iOS 17 or later, compatible with iPhone and iPad.
  • Requires no account and no sign-in, and requests no system permissions.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why do some maps show a blank grid, or photos on a few pages show a "Network Required" card?

All curriculum modules are bundled inside the App for offline use, but the map basemaps in "Taiwan Engineering Landscapes" require an internet connection to download map tiles from the National Land Surveying and Mapping Center (MOI) and Esri map servers, and photos on a few pages are loaded from Wikimedia Commons. When offline, maps display a blank grid and photos are replaced with a "Network Required" card while all other features work normally.

Why do YouTube videos and external interactive websites open separately instead of playing directly on the page?

The App does not open any external web pages. YouTube videos and external interactive websites are not embedded in the page; an information card is shown instead. Tapping a link to an external website only shows the message “App 版不開啟外部網站” (the App does not open external websites). Teachers can open these sites on a computer in class if needed.

Are my answer records in the modules uploaded to a teacher's spreadsheet?

No, the App version does not upload any answer records. To protect privacy, the App version actively blocks at the web layer features that send answer records to spreadsheets (Google Apps Script), form submissions, Google Sign-In and Google Drive, multiplayer connections, original website analytics/tracking scripts, Google Fonts, and web geolocation requests (always denied), and hides the teacher dashboard entry.

How can I save, print, or share worksheets or my work?

Inside the modules, "Print" generates a PDF, "Download" generates a file, and "Share" passes text or a URL. All three open the iOS system share sheet so you can choose to save to "Files", send via AirDrop, print, or share with others. "Copy" only places content into the system clipboard. The App itself never sends anything automatically.

Where are my learning progress and answer records stored, and how do I clear them?

Opened/completed modules, favorites (up to 300 items), recently opened items, in-page interaction completion counts, and web module answer progress stored in the system WebKit local web storage all remain exclusively on this device without iCloud sync, and the developer cannot read your data. Deleting the App deletes all data completely.
