LivingTech Interactive Lab Privacy Policy

Applies to: iPhone and iPad App "LivingTech Interactive Lab" (Chinese name: 生科互動實驗室, Bundle ID: tw.livingtech.books)　Effective Date: October 28, 2026　Developer: Henry Chao (Individual)

"LivingTech Interactive Lab" brings together interactive modules from five living technology and engineering curriculum websites (Junior High Living Technology, Senior High Living Technology, Senior High Engineering Design, Taiwan Engineering Landscapes, and Circuit Practice Labs) along with a Python Coding Dojo in a single App, providing topic categories, search, favorites, recently opened items, and learning progress through a native interface. All curriculum content and the Python execution environment (Pyodide) are bundled inside the App for offline use, and code runs strictly on this device. This App requires no account and no sign-in, asks for only one system permission (and only when you choose "Save Image"), contains no ads and no analytics or tracking SDKs, does not use iCloud sync, and does not sell or share any data with third parties. The developer has no servers and cannot read user data. The optional "Hand in to teacher" feature is off by default; only after a teacher sets it up on the device are answers that students hand in sent to the teacher's own Google Sheet (see below).

On-Device Data and Offline Execution

  • Local Learning Records: Opened and completed modules, favorites (up to 300 items), recently opened items, and in-page interaction completion counts are stored exclusively on this device.
  • Web Module Answer Progress: Each web module's own answer progress is stored in the system WebKit's local web storage and remains only on this device.
  • Bundled Content and Python Runtime: All learning materials are packaged inside the App for offline availability. The Python runtime (Pyodide) for the Python Coding Dojo is also built into the App, and programs run strictly on this device.
  • Data Retention and Deletion: This App does not use iCloud sync. The developer operates no servers and cannot read user data. Apart from answers that students hand in after a teacher turns on "Hand in to teacher", all data exists only on this device, and deleting the App deletes all data completely.

Permissions and Web-Layer Privacy Protection

  • System Permissions: The app uses only one system permission, and asks for it only when needed: the first time you choose "Save Image" in the share sheet, iOS asks whether the app may add images to Photos. The app adds only the image you chose to save and never reads your photo library. Apple Intelligence feedback runs on the device and needs no extra permission.
  • Active Web-Layer Blocking: To protect privacy, the App version actively blocks at the web layer the original websites' analytics/tracking scripts, Google Fonts, Google Sign-In and Google Drive, the websites' original features that send answer records to spreadsheets (Google Apps Script; the App keeps only the teacher-enabled "Hand in to teacher" described below), form submissions, multiplayer connections, and web geolocation requests (always denied). Teacher dashboard entry points are also hidden in the App version.

Limited Content Requiring Network Access

  • Map Basemaps: When connected to the internet, the map basemaps in "Taiwan Engineering Landscapes" download map tiles from the map servers of the National Land Surveying and Mapping Center (Ministry of the Interior) and Esri. All learning-material photos are bundled in the App.
  • Connection Information and Offline Behavior: These servers only receive standard information inherent to normal network connections (such as IP address and which image is requested); the App does not send any personal data. When offline, maps display a blank grid and all other features work as usual.

Open-Ended Questions: AI Feedback and "Hand In to Teacher"

AI feedback (on-device processing)

Some open-ended questions in this app offer an "AI feedback" button. When you tap it, the question and your answer are analyzed only on your device: devices that support Apple Intelligence use Apple's on-device language model, and other devices use rules built into the app. The analysis does not connect to the internet, and the question, your answer and the feedback are never sent to the developer, Apple or any third party, nor stored outside the app. AI feedback is only a learning hint; it may be wrong and it is not a grade.

"Hand in to teacher": syncing to the teacher's Google Sheet (optional, off by default)

  1. This feature is off by default. Students see the "Hand in to teacher" button and the class/seat fields only after a teacher, on that device, opens "About & Privacy → Teacher settings", enters the teacher passcode, enters a Google Apps Script web-app URL that the teacher deployed in their own Google account, and turns the feature on.
  2. When a student taps "Hand in to teacher", the app sends the following to the URL the teacher set: class and seat number (typed by the student, optional), a page code, question code and question text, the full answer (up to 1,000 characters), analysis marks produced by the app (completeness, specificity, relevance and a one-sentence note, plus whether they came from on-device AI or the built-in rules), the time the answer was written on the device, and a random submission ID that is different for every submission. The app does not send names, student ID numbers, email addresses, device identifiers, location or any advertising identifier, and it does not use cookies.
  3. The data is stored in the teacher's own Google account (Google Sheets) and is managed by the teacher and their school, subject to Google's terms of service and privacy policy. The developer does not handle, receive or have access to this data.
  4. Before sending, submissions are kept on the device in a queue (up to 200 items, 30 days); if there is no connection they are sent automatically later. When the teacher turns the feature off, any unsent submissions on the device are deleted. Class and seat numbers are kept only while the app is running and are cleared when the app is closed.
  5. Students who are minors: most users of this app are junior-high and senior-high school students. Before turning on "Hand in to teacher", schools and teachers should, in line with Taiwan's Personal Data Protection Act and school rules, explain to students (and, where required, to parents or guardians) the purpose, the data collected, how long it is kept and how it is used; the app also shows a notice on the student's screen. Students may leave the class and seat fields empty and hand in anonymously.
  6. Teacher and school responsibilities: the teacher collects and keeps this data and should set the sheet's sharing permissions carefully (not "anyone with the link can view"), use it only for teaching, and keep or delete it at the end of the term according to school rules.
  7. How to stop and delete: in "Teacher settings" the teacher can turn syncing off (which also deletes unsent submissions on the device) or tap "Reset teacher settings" to clear the URL and passcode; archiving the deployment in Google Apps Script ("Deploy → Manage deployments") stops it from receiving data immediately; data already sent can be deleted directly in the teacher's Google Sheet. Students or parents who want a submitted answer deleted should contact the teacher.

Transmission security: submissions are always sent over HTTPS to script.google.com, and the app connects only to the single URL the teacher set.

External Videos and External Websites

The App does not embed any external web pages. YouTube videos and external interactive websites are not played inside the App; an information card is shown instead. Links to external websites in the learning content (for example, further resources and textbook videos) first ask “Open the external website in Safari?”; only after the user taps “Open” does the App hand the link to Safari, leaving the App. Those websites are governed by their own privacy policies, and the App sends them no data.

Exporting, Sharing, and Email Links

  • Print, Download, Share, and Copy: Within the modules, "Print" generates a PDF, "Download" generates a file, and "Share" passes text or a URL. All three use the iOS system share sheet, allowing the user to choose whether to save to "Files", send via AirDrop, print, or share with a specific recipient. "Copy" only places content into the system clipboard. The App itself never transmits anything automatically.
  • Email Links: Email links (mailto) are handed off to the system Mail App.

Children and Student Privacy

This App includes interactive curriculum modules for junior high and senior high living technology and engineering design, making it suitable for students and general learners alike. The App requires no account and no sign-in, the developer collects no personal data, and the App actively blocks the websites' original spreadsheet answer submissions, form submissions, and multiplayer connections at the web layer. Unless a teacher turns on "Hand in to teacher", all learning progress and answer records remain solely on this device.
