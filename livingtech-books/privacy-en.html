LivingTech Interactive Lab Privacy Policy

Applies to: the iPhone and iPad app "LivingTech Interactive Lab" (Chinese name: 生科互動實驗室, bundle ID: tw.livingtech.books)　Last updated: November 13, 2026　Developer: Henry Chao (individual)

"LivingTech Interactive Lab" contains 140 interactive modules and lesson animations for junior and senior high school Living Technology and Engineering Design classes, arranged by the learning content of the Technology domain in Taiwan's 108 Curriculum Guidelines. A native interface provides a curriculum map, search, favorites, recently opened items and learning progress. The interactive modules, the first chapter of every lesson animation and the Python runtime (Pyodide) are bundled in the app, work offline, and run only on this device. The app requires no account and no sign-in, asks for one system permission (and only when you choose "Save Image"), contains no ads and no analytics or tracking SDKs, does not use iCloud sync, and does not sell or share any data with third parties. The developer has no servers and cannot read user data. The optional "Hand in to teacher" feature is off by default; only after a teacher sets it up on the device are answers that students hand in sent to the teacher's own Google Sheet (see below).

On-Device Data and Offline Use

  • Local learning records: opened and completed modules, favorites (up to 300), recently opened items, in-page interaction counts and which lesson animations you have watched are stored only on this device.
  • Answer progress inside modules: each module's own progress is kept in the system WebKit local web storage and stays on this device.
  • Bundled content and Python runtime: the interactive modules, photos and the first chapter of every lesson animation are packaged in the app and work offline. The Python runtime (Pyodide) is also bundled, and programs run only on this device.
  • Retention and deletion: the app does not use iCloud sync. The developer has no servers and cannot read user data. Apart from answers that students hand in after a teacher turns on "Hand in to teacher", all data exists only on this device. You can clear learning progress under "Mine → About & Privacy", and deleting the app deletes everything.

Permissions and Web-Layer Privacy Protection

  • System permission: the app uses one system permission and asks for it only when needed: the first time you choose "Save Image" in the share sheet, iOS asks whether the app may add images to Photos. The app adds only the image you chose to save and never reads your photo library. It does not use the camera, microphone or location. Apple Intelligence feedback runs on the device and needs no extra permission.
  • Web-layer blocking: to protect privacy, the app blocks the modules' original analytics and tracking scripts, Google Fonts, Google Sign-In and Google Drive, the modules' original feature that sent answer records to spreadsheets (the app keeps only the teacher-enabled "Hand in to teacher" described below), form submissions, multiplayer connections and web geolocation requests (always denied).

Content That Needs a Network Connection

  • Map basemaps: when online, the basemap in "Taiwan Engineering Landscapes" downloads tiles from the map servers of the National Land Surveying and Mapping Center (Ministry of the Interior, Taiwan) and Esri. Those servers receive only what any network connection carries (such as the IP address and which tile is requested); the app sends no personal data. Offline, the map shows a blank grid and everything else works as usual.
  • Tips and More learning resources: loading tip prices and opening an App Store product page connect to Apple's App Store.

Lesson Animations, YouTube and AI-Generated Content

  • First chapter in the app: the first chapter of every lesson animation is bundled in the app and plays in the system player with no network connection and no external service.
  • Full version on YouTube: the unit that contains an animation has a "Watch the full version (YouTube)" link. Only when you tap it do you leave the app, and YouTube (the YouTube app or your browser) opens the full version. From then on, YouTube's (Google's) terms of service and privacy policy apply. The app sends no data to YouTube.
  • AI-generated content: the narration voices, character animation and background music of the lesson animations are AI-generated; the scripts and teaching content are written and reviewed by the developer, who is the author of the learning materials. "AI feedback" on open-ended questions is also an automatically generated learning hint; it may be wrong and it is not a grade.

More Learning Resources (App Store Product Pages)

"Mine → More learning resources" lists other free educational apps by the same developer. "View in App Store" only opens the App Store product page; nothing is downloaded automatically. The links carry no promotional or tracking parameters, and the app does not record what you tap.

Some modules show "Related physics experiments". If the same developer's app "Physics Lab" is installed, tapping an item switches to that app and opens the matching experiment; if it is not installed, its App Store product page opens instead. Only the experiment's code is passed, with no personal data.

Tips (In-App Purchase)

"Buy the developer a coffee" under "Mine → About & Privacy" is a voluntary tip that uses Apple's In-App Purchase. A tip unlocks nothing; all content in the app is free, and students do not need to tip. Apple processes the payment. The developer never receives your payment details (such as card numbers), and the app does not contact any developer server or keep purchase records. If a child's account uses Ask to Buy, the purchase completes only after a parent approves it. For refunds, contact Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Open-Ended Questions: AI Feedback and "Hand In to Teacher"

AI feedback (on-device processing)

Some open-ended questions in this app offer an "AI feedback" button. When you tap it, the question and your answer are analyzed only on your device: devices that support Apple Intelligence use Apple's on-device language model, and other devices use rules built into the app. The analysis does not connect to the internet, and the question, your answer and the feedback are never sent to the developer, Apple or any third party, nor stored outside the app. AI feedback is only a learning hint; it may be wrong and it is not a grade.

"Hand in to teacher": syncing to the teacher's Google Sheet (optional, off by default)

  1. This feature is off by default. Students see the "Hand in to teacher" button and the class/seat fields only after a teacher, on that device, opens "About & Privacy → Teacher settings", enters the teacher passcode, enters a Google Apps Script web-app URL that the teacher deployed in their own Google account, and turns the feature on.
  2. When a student taps "Hand in to teacher", the app sends the following to the URL the teacher set: class and seat number (typed by the student, optional), a page code, question code and question text, the full answer (up to 1,000 characters), analysis marks produced by the app (completeness, specificity, relevance and a one-sentence note, plus whether they came from on-device AI or the built-in rules), the time the answer was written on the device, and a random submission ID that is different for every submission. The app does not send names, student ID numbers, email addresses, device identifiers, location or any advertising identifier, and it does not use cookies.
  3. The data is stored in the teacher's own Google account (Google Sheets) and is managed by the teacher and their school, subject to Google's terms of service and privacy policy. The developer does not handle, receive or have access to this data.
  4. Before sending, submissions are kept on the device in a queue (up to 200 items, 30 days); if there is no connection they are sent automatically later. When the teacher turns the feature off, any unsent submissions on the device are deleted. Class and seat numbers are kept only while the app is running and are cleared when the app is closed.
  5. Students who are minors: most users of this app are junior-high and senior-high school students. Before turning on "Hand in to teacher", schools and teachers should, in line with Taiwan's Personal Data Protection Act and school rules, explain to students (and, where required, to parents or guardians) the purpose, the data collected, how long it is kept and how it is used; the app also shows a notice on the student's screen. Students may leave the class and seat fields empty and hand in anonymously.
  6. Teacher and school responsibilities: the teacher collects and keeps this data and should set the sheet's sharing permissions carefully (not "anyone with the link can view"), use it only for teaching, and keep or delete it at the end of the term according to school rules.
  7. How to stop and delete: in "Teacher settings" the teacher can turn syncing off (which also deletes unsent submissions on the device) or tap "Reset teacher settings" to clear the URL and passcode; archiving the deployment in Google Apps Script ("Deploy → Manage deployments") stops it from receiving data immediately; data already sent can be deleted directly in the teacher's Google Sheet. Students or parents who want a submitted answer deleted should contact the teacher.

Transmission security: submissions are always sent over HTTPS to script.google.com, and the app connects only to the single URL the teacher set.

External Content and External Links Inside Modules

The app does not embed any external web pages. External videos and external interactive websites that were embedded in a module are replaced by an information card and are not loaded. Links to external websites inside a module (for example, further resources and sources) first ask “Open the external website in Safari?”; only after you tap “Open” does the app hand the link to Safari, leaving the app. Those websites have their own privacy policies, and the app sends them no data. Email links (mailto) are handed to the system Mail app.

Exporting and Sharing

Inside modules, "Print" creates a PDF, "Download" creates a file, and "Share" passes text or a URL. All three use the iOS share sheet, so you choose whether to save to Files, send by AirDrop, print or share with someone. "Copy" only places content on the system clipboard. The app itself never sends anything automatically.

Children and Student Privacy

The app is intended for junior and senior high school students and teachers. It requires no account and no sign-in, the developer collects no personal data, and there are no ads or tracking. Unless a teacher turns on "Hand in to teacher", all learning progress and answers stay on this device. Tips are voluntary, and students do not need to tip.
