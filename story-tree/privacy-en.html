Banyan Tree Stories — Privacy Policy

App: “大榕樹故事” (Banyan Tree Stories) for iOS　Effective: November 18, 2026　Developer: Henry Chao (individual)

Banyan Tree Stories is a story and bedtime white-noise app for children aged 3–6. All story audio is bundled in the app; listening does not require a network connection.

No account or sign-in, no ads, no third-party analytics, tracking or advertising SDKs, and no iCloud sync. Favorites, listening history and settings stay on the device. The only feature that sends data is “Anonymous listening statistics”, which is off by default and can only be turned on by a parent in the Parents area.

1. Anonymous listening statistics (parent opt-in)

  • Off by default. Turned on in Settings → Parents area, which is protected by an arithmetic question (parental gate).
  • Per-story totals only: number of plays, completions (80% or more), replays, skips within the first 30 seconds, favorites added/removed, and parent reports with optional reasons (too long, too scary, hard to understand, mispronunciation, other).
  • No identifying data: no name, account, email, device identifier, advertising identifier (IDFA), location, or timestamps of what was played.
  • No IP addresses stored: totals are sent to the developer's own endpoint hosted on Cloudflare, which stores only “date, story, metric, count”. IP addresses and connection details are not stored, and data is never shared with third parties or used for advertising.
  • Batched: counts are kept on the device and sent together every few hours.
  • Turn off any time: recording stops and unsent counts on the device are deleted.

2. Data stored on the device

Favorites, recently played stories, resume position, play counts and settings stay in the app's storage on this device, are not synced via iCloud, and cannot be read by the developer. Clear them in Settings; deleting the app removes everything.

3. Children's privacy

The app is designed for young children and collects no personal information from them. Network features and parent reports sit behind a parental gate.

4. Content and AI disclosure

Stories are adapted from public-domain sources (Aesop, the Brothers Grimm, classical Chinese texts and more) and written and reviewed by people; narration, character voices and music are AI-generated.
