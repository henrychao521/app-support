Banyan Tree Stories — Privacy Policy

App: “大榕樹故事” (Banyan Tree Stories) for iOS　Effective: November 24, 2026　Developer: Henry Chao (individual)

Banyan Tree Stories is an audio-story app for children aged 3–6. Stories bundled with the app play without a network connection; new or updated stories are downloaded from the developer's own server and then also play offline.

No account or sign-in, no ads, no third-party analytics, tracking or advertising SDKs, and no iCloud sync. Favorites, listening history and settings stay on the device. The only feature that sends data is “Anonymous listening statistics”, which is off by default and can only be turned on by a parent in the Parents area. Downloading stories sends no personal data; the server only counts how many times each story is downloaded, without knowing who downloaded it.

1. Anonymous listening statistics (parent opt-in)

  • Off by default. Turned on in Settings → Parents area, which is protected by an arithmetic question (parental gate).
  • Per-story totals only: number of plays, completions (80% or more), replays, skips within the first 30 seconds, favorites added/removed, and parent reports with optional reasons (too long, too scary, hard to understand, mispronunciation, other).
  • No identifying data: no name, account, email, device identifier, advertising identifier (IDFA), location, or timestamps of what was played.
  • No IP addresses stored: totals are sent to the developer's own endpoint hosted on Cloudflare, which stores only “date, story, metric, count”. IP addresses and connection details are not stored, and data is never shared with third parties or used for advertising.
  • Batched: counts are kept on the device and sent together every few hours.
  • Turn off any time: recording stops and unsent counts on the device are deleted.

2. Downloading story content

  • The app periodically checks the developer's own server on Cloudflare (content.henrylivingtech.com) for the story catalog and downloads new or updated story audio and illustrations, which are then stored on the device and play offline.
  • No name, account, device identifier or other personal data is sent; the server does not keep IP addresses or connection logs. Only the developer's own server is contacted, never a third-party service.
  • Download counts per story (from November 24, 2026): each time a story's audio is downloaded, the server adds 1 to that story's count for the day. It stores only “date, story, count”, with region limited to “Taiwan” or “other” (the country is derived from the connection and only this two-way label is kept; IP addresses are not stored). No device, account, time of day or anything that could identify a person is recorded, and nothing is shared with third parties. The counts are used only to see which stories are popular and decide what kinds of stories to make next.
  • If paid stories are offered in the future: when downloading a purchased story, the app sends Apple's transaction ID to the developer's server, which asks Apple to confirm the purchase before allowing the download. The transaction ID contains no name, Apple Account or payment details and is not stored.

3. Data stored on the device

Favorites, recently played stories, resume position, play counts and settings stay in the app's storage on this device, are not synced via iCloud, and cannot be read by the developer. Clear them in Settings; deleting the app removes everything.

4. Children's privacy

The app is designed for young children and collects no personal information from them. Anonymous statistics and parent reports sit behind a parental gate; story downloads only contact the developer's own server.

5. Content and AI disclosure

Stories are adapted from public-domain sources (Aesop, the Brothers Grimm, classical Chinese texts and more) and written and reviewed by people; narration, character voices, music and illustrations are AI-generated.
