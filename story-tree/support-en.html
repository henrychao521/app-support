Banyan Tree Stories — Help & Support

Banyan Tree Stories for iOS: audio stories for children aged 3–6 (Traditional Chinese, Taiwanese narration, no ads)

About

Audio stories adapted from Aesop, the Brothers Grimm and Chinese idioms, plus “Daily life and safety” stories, sorted into daytime, evening and bedtime listening, with new stories every Tuesday, Friday and Sunday. Bundled stories play offline; new stories download automatically when online and then also play offline.

Requirements

iPhone or iPad with iOS 17 or later.

FAQ

QuestionAnswer
How do I stop playback at bedtime?In the player, under “When to stop”, choose a timer (10–60 min), a number of stories, or “stop after this story”. You can let the current story finish before stopping. The moon button in the mini player also sets “stop after this story”, “stop after 2 stories (including the current one)” or a 15/30-minute timer.
How do I keep my child from tapping everything?Use “Parent lock” at the bottom of the player: it covers all buttons; press and hold the lock for 3 seconds to unlock. iOS Guided Access also works.
What is the Parents area?Settings → Parents area, protected by an arithmetic question. There you can opt in to anonymous listening statistics, turn on new-story reminders and report unsuitable stories. It closes automatically when you leave the Settings tab or the app goes to the background.
Can I play on HomePod or a Bluetooth speaker?Yes. Tap the AirPlay button in the player and choose a HomePod, Apple TV, Bluetooth speaker or a Mac with AirPlay Receiver turned on; the player shows where it is playing. Adjust volume in the player, or with the TV remote when playing to a TV.
What do anonymous statistics send?Off by default. When on, only per-story counts (plays, completions, skips, favorites) and parent reports, with no personal data. See the privacy policy.
Will favorites move to a new phone?No. Favorites and history stay on the device (no iCloud sync).

Content and AI disclosure

Stories are adapted from public-domain sources and written and reviewed by people; voices and music are AI-generated.
