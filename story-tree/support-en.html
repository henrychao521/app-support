Banyan Tree Stories — Help & Support

Banyan Tree Stories for iOS: stories and bedtime white noise for children aged 3–6 (Traditional Chinese audio)

About

More than a hundred audio stories adapted from Aesop, the Brothers Grimm, Chinese idioms and history, world fairy tales, festivals and great people, sorted into daytime, evening and bedtime listening, plus white noise and sleep music that can play along. All stories are bundled; no network needed.

Requirements

iPhone or iPad with iOS 17 or later.

FAQ

QuestionAnswer
How do I stop playback at bedtime?In the player, under “When to stop”, choose a timer (10–60 min), a number of stories, or “stop after this story”. You can let the current story finish before stopping, and choose whether white noise stops too or keeps playing for a while.
How do I keep my child from tapping everything?Use “Parent lock” at the bottom of the player: it covers all buttons; press and hold the lock for 3 seconds to unlock. iOS Guided Access also works.
What is the Parents area?Settings → Parents area, protected by an arithmetic question. There you can opt in to anonymous listening statistics and report unsuitable stories.
What do anonymous statistics send?Off by default. When on, only per-story counts (plays, completions, skips, favorites) and parent reports, with no personal data. See the privacy policy.
Will favorites move to a new phone?No. Favorites and history stay on the device (no iCloud sync).

Content and AI disclosure

Stories are adapted from public-domain sources and written and reviewed by people; voices and music are AI-generated.
