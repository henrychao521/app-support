DodgeStep Privacy Policy

Applies to the iPhone/iPad app “閃身上學” (DodgeStep, bundle ID tw.livingtech.dodgestep). Effective: November 9, 2026. Developer: Henry Chao (individual).

DodgeStep is a pixel-art arcade game: swipe left and right to deadpan “flash-step” past classmates, teachers and instructors walking toward you in a school hallway.

The app does not connect to the internet, has no accounts, no ads, and no analytics or tracking SDKs, and requests no system permissions (no camera, microphone, location or notifications). It does not collect, upload, sell or share any data. The developer cannot see any of your data.

1. Haptics and sound

The game uses the iPhone’s haptic feedback and sound effects. No permission is needed and nothing is recorded. Music and sound effects can be turned off on the title or game-over screen, and sound follows the ring/silent switch.

2. Children’s privacy

No account is required. The app collects no names, contact details or other personal information, and has no in-app purchases.
