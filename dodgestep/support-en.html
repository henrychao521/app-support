How to play

  • Hallway: swipe left/right to change lanes (or switch to “follow” control on the title screen) and avoid mops, buckets, desks and running groups.
  • Face-off: when someone walks straight at you, the camera cuts to a face-to-face standoff. They lean slightly to one side (yellow arrows) — swipe the opposite way to slip past. Same side = “awkward”; three awkward moments and you bump into each other.
  • The friend copies your move: step one way, then quickly the other. The instructor must be dodged twice.
  • Swipe up/down = move forward/back (four rows): further forward scores more but gives less reaction time; basketballs fly at your current row, so change rows to dodge.
  • Swipe down on the back row = back-step: dodges flying basketballs in the hallway, or escapes a face-off (limited uses, +1 per new area).
  • Higher best scores unlock more characters; use the arrows on the title screen.
